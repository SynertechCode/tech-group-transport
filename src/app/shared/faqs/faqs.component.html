How much does it cost to ship my vehicle?

Several factors affect the shipping cost, such as:

• The year, make, and model of the vehicle
• Whether the vehicle is running
• The time of year
• The weight of the vehicle

How long will it take to book a driver?

It typically takes about 1–3 business days to book a driver from your initial available pickup date.

However, the shipping timeline can fluctuate depending on the season and specific locations.

How long will it take to deliver my vehicle?

The average delivery time is approximately 1 day for every 300 miles.

However, factors such as the driver’s schedule, road conditions, weather, and other variables can affect actual delivery time.

Can I put personal items in my vehicle?

We recommend not placing personal items inside the vehicle, as they are not covered by insurance in case of damage or loss.

However, you may pack up to 100 lbs of personal belongings in the trunk.

In SUVs, minivans, or trucks, store items below the window line with the back seat upright. Exceeding the limit may result in additional charges.

Does someone need to be present for pick-up and delivery?

Yes, someone must be present to release the vehicle at pickup and sign the inspection report.

This person can be anyone over 18 years of age.

What is the difference between Open and Enclosed Transport?

Open Transport: Your vehicle is loaded onto a 10-car trailer, exposed to the elements — it’s the most common and affordable method.

Enclosed Transport: Your car is shipped inside a covered trailer for extra protection, ideal for classic, restored, or luxury cars.

Is my vehicle insured during transportation?

Yes. By law, all carriers must carry insurance that covers your vehicle during transit.

The driver’s insurance covers damage caused by their negligence.

How do I pay for auto shipping?

Full prepayment by credit/debit card, bank transfer, Venmo, CashApp, or PayPal is also accepted.
